Our Story
ABOUT
KARNATAKA

Karnataka — the land where ancient empires left their stone signatures across a dramatic landscape. From the granite-strewn ruins of Vijayanagara to the spice-scented hills of Kodagu, every corner holds a civilization's memory.

"Where the Western Ghats meet ancient stone — a landscape that rewrites your definition of beauty."

Home to UNESCO World Heritage Sites, the world's second largest waterfall during monsoon, pristine beaches, and biodiversity that rivals the Amazon — Karnataka is India's best-kept secret no longer.
